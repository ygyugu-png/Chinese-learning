「了」的用法

「了」is one of the most useful words in Chinese, and one of the most misunderstood. Work through the seven steps in order. Each step has a short explanation, examples, and a quick check. The full practice is in step 7.

1Use V + 了 to say an action is completed
2Use sentence-final 了 for a change or new situation
3Make negatives and questions correctly
4Know when not to use 了
STEP 1热身5 min STEP 2V + 了10 min STEP 3句尾 了10 min STEP 4否定和问句6 min STEP 5比一比10 min STEP 6不用「了」6 min STEP 7练习20 min
最重要 · KEY IDEA

「了」不是过去时 (not a past tense). It has two main jobs: ① it shows that an action is 完成 completed; ② it shows that a situation has 变化 changed. Time words like 昨天 tell you when; 了 tells you whether it's done or new.

1

热身：注意到区别了吗？

5 min

Notice the difference. Read each pair, think about what changes in meaning, then reveal.

2

V + 了：完成 Completion

10 min

了 straight after a verb tells us the action is completed. We call it 了1.

主语 + 动词（+ 补语）+ 了 + 数量 / 描述 + 宾语
SubjectVerb (+ complement)了Number / descriptionObject
我买了6个苹果
她看（完）了昨天的足球比赛
小王吃（完）了午饭
大卫学（好）了中国功夫

想做的还是做完的？ Plan or done?

你买什么？Nǐ mǎi shénme?What are you going to buy?
我买6个苹果。Wǒ mǎi liù ge píngguǒ.I'm going to buy 6 apples.
你买了什么？Nǐ mǎi le shénme?What did you buy?
我买了6个苹果。Wǒ mǎi le liù ge píngguǒ.I bought 6 apples.
Expert tip · 专家提示 ①

A bare object sounds unfinished. 我买了苹果…… makes a Chinese listener wait for more ("…and then?"). Complete the sentence in one of three ways:

• add a number or description: 我买了6个苹果。 · 她看了昨天的比赛。
• add a follow-up clause: 我买了苹果就回家了。
• use sentence-final 了 instead (step 3): 我买苹果了。

Expert tip · 专家提示 ②

With a complement, 了 goes after the complement, never in between: 我写好了。 ✓ · 我写了好。 ✗ · 她看完了那本书。 ✓

⚡ Quick check
3

句尾 了：变化 Change

10 min

了 at the end of a sentence tells us that something is new or different from before. We call it 了2. It can follow verbs, adjectives, and whole sentences.

…… + 了。（句子最后 · at the end of the sentence）

① 新情况 A new situation

下雨了。Xià yǔ le.It's started raining. (It wasn't before.)
天气冷了。Tiānqì lěng le.It's got cold.
小王不在家，他去图书馆了。Xiǎo Wáng bú zài jiā, tā qù túshūguǎn le.Xiao Wang isn't home. He's gone to the library.
对不起，我来晚了。Duìbuqǐ, wǒ lái wǎn le.Sorry, I'm late.

② 会 / 能 + V + 了: “now I can”

我会开车了。Wǒ huì kāichē le.I can drive now. (I couldn't before.)
我现在能看懂中文菜单了！Wǒ xiànzài néng kàn dǒng Zhōngwén càidān le!Now I can read Chinese menus!

③ 不 + V + 了: “not any more”, a change of plan

今天晚上，他不来晚会。He isn't coming to the party tonight. (just a fact)
今天晚上，他不来晚会了。He's not coming after all. (He was going to come; now he has changed his plan.)

④ 语气 Emphasis: set patterns

这个电脑太贵了！Zhège diànnǎo tài guì le!This computer is way too expensive! (太 + adj. + 了)
我早就想学中国功夫了！Wǒ zǎo jiù xiǎng xué Zhōngguó gōngfu le!I've wanted to learn kung fu for ages!
明天要考试了。Míngtiān yào kǎoshì le.The exam is tomorrow! (要……了 = about to happen)
Expert tip · 专家提示

A useful test: if you can add “now” or “already” in English (It's now raining; I can drive now; he's already gone), sentence-final 了 is probably right.

⚡ Quick check
4

否定和问句 Negatives & questions

6 min

This is where most mistakes happen. One rule: 没 and 了 never go together for the same action.

没（有）+ 动词　→　不用「了」
我看了电影。
我没（有）看电影。
我买了三本书。
我没（有）买书。
我没看了电影。
我不看了电影。

问句 Questions and short answers

QuestionYesNot yet
你吃饭了吗？吃了。还没（吃）。
你做作业了没有？做了。还没做呢。
他来了没有？来了。还没来。
Expert tip · 专家提示

还没……呢 (“not yet”) is extremely common in speech: 我还没吃呢。 It suggests you will do it soon.

⚡ Quick check
5

比一比 Compare: 了₁ vs 了₂

10 min

Same words, different message.

她去哪儿了？Where is she? (Where has she gone?)
她去上海了。She's gone to Shanghai. (She's there now.)
你去了哪儿？Where did you go? (Where have you been?)
我去了上海。I went to Shanghai. (I'm back now.)

他去书店买词典：了在哪儿？ Click each sentence

一个“了”还是两个“了”？ One 了 or two?

我学了两年中文。
I studied Chinese for two years. The two years are finished; maybe I stopped.
我学了两年中文了。
I've been studying Chinese for two years (so far), and I'm still studying. The second 了 brings it up to now.

Main meaningAction completedChange, new situation
Number before the object✓ 她买了6个苹果。✗ 她买6个苹果了。
After adjectives—✓ 天冷了。
About the future✓ 下了课我去找你。✓ 明天要考试了。

6

什么时候不用「了」？

6 min

了 ≠ past tense. Many sentences about the past have no 了, and some sentences about the future do.

① 过去的习惯 Habits: 每天、常常、经常、以前……
以前我每天跑步。
以前我每天跑步了。

我以前天天坐公共汽车，现在我每天开车去大学。 Both halves describe habits, so no 了.

② 过去的状态 States: 是、在、喜欢、觉得、知道 (describing how things were)
昨天天气很好。
昨天天气很好了。
小时候我很喜欢吃糖。
小时候我很喜欢了吃糖。

But these verbs can take sentence-final 了 when there's a change: 我知道了！ (Now I know / Got it!) · 我喜欢上他了。 (I've fallen for him.)

③ 几个动作 Several actions in a row: 了 usually goes on the last one

她站起来，打开门，跑了出去。

She stood up, opened the door and ran out.

了 can be used about the future

明天下了课我就去找你。　你吃了饭以后快去看书，明天要考试了。

“Once class is over tomorrow, I'll come to find you.” 了 marks the first action as completed before the second one, even in the future.

Describing a scene: your choice

在昨天的晚会上，小王唱歌，我跳舞，大卫表演中国功夫。

When you paint a picture of what was going on, rather than reporting completed results, you can leave 了 out.

总结 Summary

① 完成 V + 了我买了6个苹果。
② 变化 ……了。下雨了。我会开车了。
③ 否定 没 + V我没看电影。（没有了！）
④ 问句 ……了吗？你吃饭了吗？—还没呢。
