声调Pinyin and tones

Two different “i” sounds. After z c s zh ch sh r, the i is not “ee” — it is a buzzed continuation of the consonant (zī, cì, shì). After j q x, the i is a clear “ee” (jī, xiǎo). Say zì 字 and jì 记 one after the other and feel the difference.

频率pín lǜ · how often? frequency words

Word order: the frequency word sits after the subject, before the verb. 有时候 can also start the sentence: 有时候我喝咖啡。

常常 or 经常? Both mean “often”. In the negative say 不常 — never 不常常.

……了没有？Asking “did you…?”

Two ways to ask about the past

不 or 没? Now, habits and the future use 不: 你去不去？ Things that did or didn’t happen use 没: 你去没去？

Your turn: ask the question, then answer it

Answer with the verb, not “yes” or “no”: 吃了, 没吃.

不过bú guò · but, however

Same meaning as 但是 and 可是, but a little lighter and more conversational.

Tone change: 不 is normally bù, but before a 4th tone it becomes bú: bú guò, bú shì, bú qù.

Translate with 不过, then check.

以前 · 以后yǐ qián before, in the past · yǐ hòu after, later

以前
🐸

以前他是一只青蛙。

Yǐ qián tā shì yì zhī qīng wā.

He used to be a frog.

现在
🤴

现在他是王子。

Xiàn zài tā shì wáng zǐ.

Now he is a prince.

以后
💍

以后他要和公主结婚。

Yǐ hòu tā yào hé gōng zhǔ jié hūn.

Later he will marry the princess.

Bonus: event + 以前/以后 = before/after it: 下课以后 after class, 吃饭以前 before eating.

因为……所以……yīn wèi … suǒ yǐ · because … so …

In English we say “because” or “so”. Chinese happily uses both in one sentence.

You can also use just one half

因为……，所以我学中文。
