就 还是 才？

Earlier or later than expected? Easy or hard? Chinese tells you with one word.

第一部分 · Part 1

比想的早，还是比想的晚？

Earlier than expected, or later than expected?

就
“already / as early as”: earlier or easier than expected
时间 + 就 + V (O) + 了
  • 他19岁就结婚了。Tā shíjiǔ suì jiù jiéhūn le.He got married as early as 19.
才
“not until”: later or harder than expected
时间 + 才 + V (O)　no 了
  • 他40岁才结婚。Tā sìshí suì cái jiéhūn.He didn't get married until 40.

Both 就 and 才 go after the time word and before the verb.

第二部分 · Part 2

就是 = exactly, precisely

就 can also add emphasis: “this is the one”.

  • 他就是王老师。Tā jiù shì Wáng lǎoshī.He is (none other than) Teacher Wang.
  • 这里就是杜伦大学。Zhèlǐ jiù shì Dùlún Dàxué.This is Durham University (right here).
  • 这本书就是你的书。Zhè běn shū jiù shì nǐ de shū.This book is exactly your book.

第四部分 · Part 4

不只是时间：多少 / 多长

Not only time: 就 = it only took a little; 才 = it took a lot.

📗 他学了一本中文书，就说得很流利了。 Tā xué le yì běn Zhōngwén shū, jiù shuō de hěn liúlì le. He studied just one book and was already fluent.
📕📕📕📕📕📕📕📕📕📕 我学了10本中文书，才说得流利。 Wǒ xué le shí běn Zhōngwén shū, cái shuō de liúlì. I had to study ten books before I became fluent.
  • 他家离大学很近，走路一刻钟就到了。Tā jiā lí dàxué hěn jìn, zǒulù yí kè zhōng jiù dào le.His home is close to the university: only 15 minutes' walk.
第五部分 · Part 5

就……了，才……（没有了）

The 了 rule for things that have happened

就 usually comes with 了

她7点半就来。
她7点半就来了。

才 does not take 了

他9点半才来了。
他9点半才来。

Both go after the time word, before the verb

他就7点半来了。
他7点半就来了。
他才来9点半。
他9点半才来。
第六部分 · Part 6

课堂活动：你今天早还是晚？

Class activity: early or late today?

  1. Tell your partner what time you usually do something: 我平常……点起床 / 吃早饭 / 到学校。
  2. Then say what time you did it today, using 就……了 or 才.
  3. Your partner reports to the class: Emma今天……点就……了 / 才……。
我平常八点起床，今天六点就起床了！Emma
我平常七点起床，今天十点才起床。Tom
