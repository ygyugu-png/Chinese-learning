和 还是 也？

“and”, “also”, “too”: which one do I use in Chinese?

1 学一学 2 和 3 也 4 English “and” 5 课堂活动 6 练习 7 总结
第一部分 · Part 1

一个连名词，一个在动词前面

One joins nouns. The other goes in front of the verb.

和
“and”: joins people and things
名词 A + 和 + 名词 B
  • 我和她wǒ hé tāme and her
  • 咖啡和茶kāfēi hé chácoffee and tea
也
“also / too”: goes before the verb
主语 + 也 + 动词 / 形容词
  • 我也是学生。wǒ yě shì xuéshēng.I am also a student.
  • 她也很忙。tā yě hěn máng.She is busy too.
第二部分 · Part 2

和 hé

How to use 和

① 和 joins nouns and pronouns

  • 我和我妹妹都是学生。Wǒ hé wǒ mèimei dōu shì xuéshēng.My younger sister and I are both students.
  • 我喜欢猫和狗。Wǒ xǐhuan māo hé gǒu.I like cats and dogs.

② With three or more things, use 、 and put 和 before the last one

  • 我学中文、法语和历史。Wǒ xué Zhōngwén, Fǎyǔ hé lìshǐ.I study Chinese, French and history.

③ 和 + person + 一起 = (together) with someone

  • 我常常和朋友一起去咖啡店。Wǒ chángcháng hé péngyou yìqǐ qù kāfēidiàn.I often go to the café with friends.

④ 和 does NOT join two sentences, or two actions done one after the other

我很忙和我很累。
我很忙，也很累。
我去超市和买了水果。
我去超市买了水果。

For “and” between two sentences, use a comma, or a comma + 也.

第三部分 · Part 3

也 yě

How to use 也

① 也 goes after the subject and before the verb or adjective

也我是英国人。
我也是英国人。
我是也英国人。
我也是英国人。

English “too” can go at the end (“I'm British too”). 也 never goes at the beginning or at the end.

② Different people, same thing

  • 他是英国人，我也是。Tā shì Yīngguó rén, wǒ yě shì.He is British, and so am I.
  • A：我喜欢喝茶。　B：我也喜欢！Wǒ xǐhuan hē chá. — Wǒ yě xǐhuan!I like tea. — Me too!

③ Same person, two things

  • 我喜欢看书，也喜欢听音乐。Wǒ xǐhuan kàn shū, yě xǐhuan tīng yīnyuè.I like reading, and I also like listening to music.
  • 我会说英语，也会说一点儿中文。Wǒ huì shuō Yīngyǔ, yě huì shuō yìdiǎnr Zhōngwén.I can speak English, and also a little Chinese.

④ 也 goes before 不, 很 and 都

  • 他不吃肉，我也不吃。Tā bù chī ròu, wǒ yě bù chī.He doesn't eat meat, and neither do I.
  • 今天也很冷。Jīntiān yě hěn lěng.Today is cold too.
  • 我们也都是学生。Wǒmen yě dōu shì xuéshēng.We are all students too.

⑤ 和 and 也 together

  • 我和Tom是英国人，Emma也是。Wǒ hé Tom shì Yīngguó rén, Emma yě shì.Tom and I are British, and so is Emma.
第四部分 · Part 4

English “and” ≠ always 和

English uses “and” almost everywhere. Chinese doesn't.

EnglishWhat is joined?Chinese
tea and coffeetwo things茶和咖啡
my mum and Itwo people我和我妈妈
I'm tired and hungry.two descriptions我很累，也很饿。
I like tea, and I also like coffee.two sentences, same person我喜欢茶，也喜欢咖啡。
He's a student, and so am I.two people, same thing他是学生，我也是。
I went to the shop and bought fruit.one action after another我去商店买了水果。(no word needed)
第五部分 · Part 5

课堂活动：我也是！

Class activity: Me too!

  1. One student says a true sentence about themselves: 我喜欢…… / 我会…… / 我不……
  2. Anyone it's true for stands up and says 我也……！
  3. The class makes a 和 sentence about two of the people standing: A和B都……
我喜欢踢足球。Tom
我也喜欢踢足球！Emma
Tom和Emma都喜欢踢足球。全班 · the whole class

总结 · Summary

记住！

Remember!

和 = and
Joins people and things: 我和你, 茶和咖啡.
也 = also / too
Subject + 也 + verb: 我也是。
Two sentences?
Not 和. Use a comma, or comma + 也.
Order
也不 · 也很 · 也都
